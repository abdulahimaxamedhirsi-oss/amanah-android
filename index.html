Common questions

Why is my transfer still processing?

We’re moving your money and running safety checks. You’ll get a notification when it finishes. If it stays in Processing or Under review longer than you expect, send us a request below with the reference number from the transfer details.

Why can’t I send money yet?

Sending unlocks once your identity check is approved. You can see the status on Home. If we need more from you, we’ll tell you what and why.

Where do I see fees and the exchange rate?

On the review screen, before you confirm. Nothing is sent until you confirm. Quotes and rates expire after a short time, and you’ll need to get a new one.

I lost my phone. What do I do?

Contact us straight away so we can secure your account. If you’re still signed in on another device, you can sign the lost one out under Security.
